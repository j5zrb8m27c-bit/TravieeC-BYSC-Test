TravieeC BYSC V1.6.5 — Release Notes
Date: 2026-08-20

V1.6.5 is a stabilization release. It introduces one authoritative 8-hour capacity boundary, one canonical Review estimate state, one Scheduling-owned appointment date, and one pre-authorization renderer.

Corrected:
- <=8.0 labor hours uses standard scheduling; >8.0 hours uses Split/Extended routing.
- Furniture and Electrical multi-item Review is canonical, item-isolated, quantity-editable, and removable.
- Unknown Review work is no longer represented as $0; known portions remain visible.
- Electrical diagnostic affected-location count is retained as scope information and no longer creates Review by itself.
- Furniture qualifiers disclose sequentially and upstream changes invalidate only the affected item.
- Preferred Day was removed from Address/Contact; Scheduling owns the appointment date.
- Same-day scheduling retains the Pacific-time 90-minute target with 5-minute tolerance (85-minute minimum), including intersection with approved After-Hours windows.
- Pre-authorization renders appointment, scope, qualifiers, labor, estimate state, and deposit information once.
- Quick Fix primary now renders one Remove control and retains pricing privacy.
- Approved Quick Fix and Standard Quick Fix catalog entries and customer boundaries were added.
- Refrigerator water-filter replacement conditionally collects filter/model availability.

Preserved:
- V1.6.4 pricing rules and approved repetition curve.
- Price efficiency remains separate from labor.
- Painting coat effects already present in the approved estimator.
- Vanity replacement photo-review rule.
- Authorization failure/retry and stable Request ID behavior.

No shared-visit cross-task pricing formula was invented.
