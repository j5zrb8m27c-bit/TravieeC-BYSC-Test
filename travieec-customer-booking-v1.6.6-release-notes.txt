TravieeC BYSC V1.6.6 — Release Notes
Date: 2026-08-21

V1.6.6 is a narrow stabilization release covering Split completion, Review quantity integrity, qualifier propagation, terminal confirmation, and Quick Fix state reconstruction.

Corrected:
- Verified Split/Multi-Day schedules now expose and complete a functional authorization action.
- Capacity authorization eligibility records required labor, selected windows/capacity, shortfall, Review state, and the exact eligibility reason.
- Quick Fix Review quantities 1–20 and 20+ now use the same canonical quantity as the title and estimator.
- Customer-facing “known estimate” and other internal estimator wording were removed.
- Electrical Diagnostic required selectors start blank and its customer qualifiers propagate through Confirmed.
- Five affected Electrical locations remain diagnostic scope information and do not independently trigger Review.
- Furniture item Review editors remain open through recalculation and close only with Done.
- Booking Confirmed is terminal; Back is removed after Request ID creation.
- Refrigerator filter procurement/model details propagate through Review, pre-authorization, and Confirmed.
- A single Quick Fix governed by the minimum is described as included in the configured service-call minimum.
- Quick Fix Back navigation reconstructs the entire catalog from canonical state.

Preserved:
- Global 8-hour capacity threshold and capacity arithmetic.
- Bundle Efficiency formula and labor separation.
- V1.6.5 estimator behavior and approved pricing rules.
- Authorization failure/retry, Scheduling date authority, same-day rules, and past-date protections.

No pricing policy or Bundle Efficiency redesign was introduced.
